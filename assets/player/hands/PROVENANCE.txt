First-person grip hands

Source: MakeHuman Community system assets (CC0), including the
middleage_lightskinned_male skin and MakeHuman human base mesh.
Official source: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
MakeHuman output rights: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/makehuman/faq/are_makehuman_files_free.html
Accessed: 25 September 2026. The public downloads required no account or payment.

Adaptation: Extracted both hands from the 53-bone MakeHuman contact prototype,
curled finger joints into a Sten grip pose, cut at the wrists, retained skin UVs,
and exported separate left/right GLB meshes with embedded skin texture.
Cloth cuffs and sleeves are original procedural game geometry, not MakeHuman assets.

Editable hand source: source/grip_hands_working.blend. Its two posed hand meshes
and skin images are packed in the Blender file. The production GLBs are
hand_left_grip.glb and hand_right_grip.glb. The extraction and pose recipe is
tools/art/weapon_pose_hands.py; the standalone source writer is
tools/art/weapon_hand_source.py. The extraction recipe reads the separately
authored MakeHuman character source while the standalone hand source remains
editable without that character file.

These hands are an initial first-person fit. They do not establish a guard or
player faction, a final costume, or any historical uniform identity.
