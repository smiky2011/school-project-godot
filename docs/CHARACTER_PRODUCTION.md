# Character production

Status: three clothed human visuals are integrated. The upstairs contact and shelter scout remain noncolliding, invulnerable interaction targets. Guards retain the existing AI, vision, collider, damage and corpse-discovery rules; their presentation now uses a rigged in-place walk instead of the earlier stride morphs. These are provisional visual assets for a playable mission, not a finished character art pipeline.

## Candidate and rights

- MakeHuman Community's [MPFB 2.0.17](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/docs/getting_started.html) generates the base human and can rig attached clothes in Blender. MPFB is an authoring tool under GPL-3.0-or-later; its extension is not included in the game package. The [MakeHuman core output](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/makehuman/faq/are_makehuman_files_free.html) and the selected system assets are CC0.
- The contact's suit is Margaret Toigo's `toigo_male_double-breasted_suit` from the [Suits 01 pack](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/suits01.html), listed there as CC0. It has been recolored and checked in the actual upstairs room.
- The selected skin, shoes, short hair, and revised field shirt/trousers and boots come from the [MakeHuman system assets pack](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html), which lists those assets as CC0. Selected originals are retained under `source/selected_assets/`; edited textures are retained beside the editable Blender files.

## Rigged guard locomotion (26 September 2026)

`tools/art/build_guard_locomotion.py` starts from the same CC0 `source/guard_field_grip.blend`, freezes its selected body proportions, applies the hidden-face masks in rest space, and keeps all eight visible meshes weighted to the 53-bone `ContactRig`. The resulting editable `source/guard_field_locomotion.blend` reopens with both `GuardIdle` and `GuardWalk` actions and no missing images. The separate `runtime/guard_field_animated.glb` contains one skin, eight meshes, and both clips. The earlier morph GLB and source remain for comparison. No new third-party material was acquired.

The walk cycles in place. `field_body_presentation.gd` blends idle and walk from actual horizontal velocity; the guard's pathfinding, collider, hit rules, and authored low-ready hand position are unchanged. Blender samples measured a 0.781 m ankle sweep per step. At the real 2.0 m/s patrol speed, 1.25-times playback yields about 0.8 m of actor travel per step, close to that sweep. Both hand bone positions stayed fixed across the sampled gait, preserving their relationship to the actor-mounted Sten. The pelvis moved about 8 mm vertically. This is an authored gait without planted-foot IK, so some residual skating on uneven ground remains possible.

The new Godot 4.7.2 Metal [five-frame review](media/guard_rigged_gait_review.png) shows idle, left contact, mid-swing, right contact, and the settled corpse; no hidden body geometry appeared. `guard_regression.gd` passed 31 checks and `rendered_guard_review.gd` passed with measured 2.0 m/s movement and a changing thigh bone pose. This is a controlled visual probe, not a human mission playthrough or final character-art acceptance.
