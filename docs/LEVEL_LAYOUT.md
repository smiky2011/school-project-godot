# Town Level Blockout

**Status:** Implemented and traversed in two rendered no-kill routes, including one run from the locally packaged Mac game. The user confirmed a fictional European town in 1944; no country, month, or faction is fixed. The dimensions, guard placement, and visual treatment are Astra-approved implementation choices within that scope, not separate user-confirmed historical claims. Human-played mission duration remains unverified. See the [topdown map](media/town_layout.svg).

## Implemented space

Coordinates are metres; negative Z is north. The town spans approximately 130 m east–west by 410 m south–north. Entry is at `(-48,198)`, the contact stands upstairs in the north-central requisitioned residence at `(4.6,3.27,-28.2)`, and the protected extraction scout stands in the northern shelter at `(44,0.06,-198)`. The retained central courtyard has a stone pump landmark. Five staggered building bands in each added district form 6–9 m streets, narrow plot alleys, small yards, and roofed walk-throughs. Functional signs identify major junctions without assigning a nation or unit.

The residence has a collision-backed ramp, upper floor, open entrance, and reachable contact. The scout shelter has walls and a canopy but its noncolliding scout remains directly interactable. Buildings and low walls block sight and bullets as well as movement. The outer boundaries prevent leaving the blockout. The only imported objects are [CC0 Poly Haven samples](ASSET_PROVENANCE.md): two stone/plaster diffuse maps, one restricted muddy approach patch, and a small colliding crate away from the required route. Materials and primitive facades are functional blockout art, not a locked architectural style.

The main street and courtyard offer a short, exposed line past the pump. Western lanes give a longer covered approach and a rear exit. An eastern yard and damaged building passage offer another connection. A roofed **southern service passage** at x≈-32.5, z≈103–119 links plot alleys between the western patrol and middle sentry streets; its opening is 4.6 m wide with at least 3 m under the lintels. A first rendered attempt crossing the exposed south street at z=130 died under patrol fire, so this connecting passage was added rather than weakening guards. The passage and the western rear lane are both traversable without a kill gate or forced wait.

Eight guards initially cover the three districts: three in the original core, three in the south, and two in the north. Securing the packet triggers one finite four-guard reinforcement contingent at the courtyard, residence crossing, workers' junction, and north edge. Reinforcements make the exposed intersections riskier while western/eastern bypasses remain physically open. Guard hearing, progressive sight, searching, local alerts, corpse discovery, and bullet occlusion are covered by synthetic regression checks; the two rendered routes below establish full-loop traversal. Other street choices and encounter balance remain open to human playtesting.
