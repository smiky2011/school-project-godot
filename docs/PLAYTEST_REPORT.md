## Pacing and feel

The current 3:23 visual-build route and older 3:22 covered and 3:28 direct-west completions are shorter than the intended roughly ten-minute mission. They are measured by the in-game mission clock; automated control ran without per-waypoint tool waits, with continuous forward motion and little observation. They do not establish how long an unaided first-time human playthrough would take. Do not lengthen the game with artificial waits merely to meet the target. The new packaged run averaged 119 FPS across one-second samples, with an 8.373 ms median and 11.155 ms 95th-percentile render-frame interval. Four >50 ms intervals followed test screenshot saves immediately; see [QA_REPORT.md](QA_REPORT.md) for the measurement boundary. The older packaged run averaged 130 FPS, with a 7.672 ms median and 9.691 ms 95th-percentile interval.

The visual build now uses modeled houses, surface materials, a textured Sten and clothed contact, guard and scout. Other town pieces and animation remain provisional. Cover choices, readable sightlines and the connected indoor contact path matter more to this vertical slice than final art. First-time human pacing remains open for later iteration.
